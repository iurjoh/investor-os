# Investor OS V1 — Private Beta Tester Guide

## Purpose

This guide is for people testing the first sellable version of Investor OS. The goal is to identify usability problems, calculation errors, unclear instructions, and missing features before wider release.

## What to test

Use a copy of the Investor OS V1 spreadsheet and try to complete these tasks without assistance:

1. Open **START HERE** and understand what the workbook does.
2. Configure a portfolio in **SETUP**.
3. Add at least 5 assets in **ASSETS**.
4. Record deposits, buys, sells, dividends, fees, and withdrawals in **TRANSACTIONS**.
5. Enter prices and FX rates in **PRICES**.
6. Enter dividend information in **DIVIDENDS**.
7. Check the calculated positions in **HOLDINGS**.
8. Review **ALLOCATION** by asset type, sector, region/country, and currency.
9. Set goals and review progress in **GOALS**.
10. Review **DASHBOARD** and **CHECKS**.

## Test with real-world data

If comfortable doing so, use a small representative portfolio or a copy of historical transactions. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, broker credentials, or other sensitive information into the workbook or feedback form.

## What to report

For every problem, report:

- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened.
- The sheet and cell involved, if known.
- The data or steps that triggered the problem.
- A screenshot when useful.

## Success criteria

A beta tester should be able to complete the main workflow without live assistance, understand the key calculations, identify missing inputs through **CHECKS**, and explain the main dashboard figures.

## Important scope

Investor OS V1 is an educational portfolio-organization tool. It is not a broker, automated trading system, tax-filing service, or personalized financial-advice service. AI features, when introduced, must not replace the deterministic spreadsheet calculations.
