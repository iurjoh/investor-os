# Investor OS V1 — Private Beta Plan

## Objective
Validate that a new investor can set up and use the spreadsheet without live assistance, and determine whether the product provides enough value to justify payment.

## Tester profile

Start with 5–10 testers who have basic spreadsheet literacy and some experience tracking investments. Prefer a mix of dividend-focused and general long-term investors.

## Tester workflow

1. Open `START HERE`.
2. Review the fictional sample portfolio.
3. Make a backup copy.
4. Enter a small real or fictional portfolio.
5. Add at least one buy, one sell and one dividend.
6. Enter current prices and FX rates.
7. Review `HOLDINGS`, `ALLOCATION`, `GOALS` and `DASHBOARD`.
8. Resolve any warnings in `CHECKS`.
9. Complete the feedback questionnaire.

## Success criteria

- Setup completed without live assistance.
- No unexplained formula errors.
- Users understand the difference between cost basis and market value.
- Users can reconcile dividends.
- Users understand the purpose of `CHECKS`.
- At least 3 testers say they would use the product monthly.
- At least 2 testers indicate willingness to pay.

## Pricing hypothesis

Initial beta options:
- Free in exchange for structured feedback, or
- 49–99 SEK symbolic beta price.

Potential public tiers:
- Starter: 149 SEK
- Pro: 299 SEK
- Future membership: 59 SEK/month

These are hypotheses to validate, not final prices.

## Feedback priorities

Prioritize issues in this order:
1. Incorrect calculations
2. Data-loss or integrity risks
3. Confusing setup/input workflow
4. Dashboard clarity
5. Missing high-value features
6. Visual polish

## Beta gate

Do not move to public launch until tester feedback has been reviewed and calculation integrity remains intact after fixes.
