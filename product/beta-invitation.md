# Investor OS V1 — Private Beta Invitation

## Purpose

Invite a small group of testers to use Investor OS V1 and provide structured feedback before wider distribution.

## Invitation

**Investor OS V1 — Private Beta**

I’m testing an early version of Investor OS, a spreadsheet-based tool for organizing an investment portfolio, tracking holdings and dividends, and understanding portfolio allocation.

I’m looking for a small number of people to test it with their own portfolio or with the included sample data.

### What I need from you

Please use the workbook through the main workflow and then complete the short feedback form.

You do not need to be an investing professional. Feedback about confusing steps, missing information, calculations, layout, and usefulness is valuable.

### Important

Investor OS V1 is an educational portfolio-organization tool. It does not provide personalized investment advice, automated trading, tax filing, or guaranteed returns.

### Test flow

1. Open `START HERE`.
2. Complete `SETUP`.
3. Add or review assets.
4. Enter transactions and dividends.
5. Enter prices and FX rates.
6. Review `HOLDINGS`, `ALLOCATION`, `GOALS`, `DASHBOARD`, and `CHECKS`.
7. Report anything confusing or incorrect.
8. Submit the beta feedback form.

### Feedback form

The feedback form supports Português (Brasil), English, and Svenska. The respondent chooses the language at the beginning.

Form: https://form.jotform.com/262573965168067

### Time required

Approximately 20–30 minutes for a first pass, depending on whether you use sample data or enter a real portfolio.

Please do not share private account credentials, broker passwords, or other sensitive information during the test.
