## Status definitions

- **Not started:** Tester has not begun.
- **In progress:** Tester has received the workbook and started the workflow.
- **Completed:** Core workflow and feedback form completed.
- **Blocked:** Tester cannot complete a required workflow because of a product issue.
- **Dropped:** Tester will not complete the beta.

## Minimum beta evidence

Before public release, aim to have:

- 5–10 completed external testers.
- At least one complete setup-to-dashboard workflow per tester.
- Feedback collected from each completed tester.
- All calculation regressions resolved.
- No known blocker affecting the core workflow.
- Usability issues triaged and either fixed or explicitly accepted.

## Issue triage

Classify every reported problem:

1. **Blocker** — prevents core use or produces materially incorrect financial output. Fix before release.
2. **High** — major confusion, broken workflow, or important incorrect behavior. Fix before release where practical.
3. **Medium** — noticeable usability or documentation issue. Fix or document workaround.
4. **Low** — cosmetic or minor improvement. May move to V1.1.

## Privacy rule

The repository should contain process documentation and anonymized tester IDs only. Keep contact details, raw questionnaire responses, and other personal information outside the public repository.
